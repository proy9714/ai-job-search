## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "research", "applied research", "R&D", "prototype", "explore new approaches", "innovation"
- "evidence-based", "data-driven decisions", "experimentation", "benchmarking"
- "work-life balance", "flexible hours", "sustainable pace", "hybrid / remote", "30 days vacation"
- "cross-functional collaboration", "learning culture", "conference budget"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "fast-paced", "high-pressure", "hustle", "wear many hats", "work hard play hard"
- "on-call", "24/7 support", "weekend availability", "tight deadlines"
- Roles dominated by maintenance, ticket queues or support of legacy systems with little new development
- Early-stage startup signals of long hours (e.g. "we're a family", "whatever it takes")

## Management Style Preferences
